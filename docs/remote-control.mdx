<Callout type="info" title= "Remote Controller and Receiver">
Instructions for the FLYSKY FS-i6X controller with the FS-ia6B receiver using PPM
</Callout>

## Binding controller with receiver

1. Disconnect the receiver from power (unplug from pixhawk) and turn off remote controller
2. Plug in the binding wire into the receiver into the spot labeled B/VCC
3. Plug the pixhawk back into the receiver in the PPM/CH1 slot (signal on top, ground on bottom) 
4. The receiver should now be blinking red
5. Press and hold the bind button on the remote controller and while holding, turn the controller on
6. The controller should show a message, beep, and then return to the normal home screen with 3 status lines on the screen
7. Turn off the controller, disconnect power from the receiver, remove the bind plug, and finally reconnect the receiver to the pixhawk
8. The controller should now be bound to the receiver and you shouldn't have to do this again for a while! 

## Modifying settings on the controller

The controller has a weird way of saving settings. You have to press and hold the **CANCEL** button to confirm.
